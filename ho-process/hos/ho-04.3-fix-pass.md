# ho-04.3 — Third fix pass for Transcript

ho-04x run 3 returned ho-04 with one Must-fix finding and two Notes, the third Return on ho-04. The standing checkpoint stopped the build; the practitioner ruled on 2026-11-05 that the fault was in the fix-pass specs, not the brief or the builder, and opened this fourth pass. Both earlier specs prescribed a check at the end of a feed while promising that no feed end matters: ho-04.1 a trim, ho-04.2 an eviction, each justified by a claim that turned out false. This spec prescribes no moment. It states the invariant, names the one place the code still breaks it, and tests equivalence under every partition of the bytes. ho-04.2's Results get a one-line note pointing here.

## Problem

From `ho-process/reviews/ho-04x-report-3.md`, confirmed by the orchestrator against the code at `4028969c97e7d4154c590693e5d1eb3d8863d0ac`:

- **M1.** `feed` calls `evict()` after its loop, so the 10 MiB ceiling is checked at the end of every feed as well as at commit. An overwrite can shrink the line in progress: `put` replaces a whole multibyte character with one byte. So with ten lines one byte under the ceiling, `é` takes the total one byte over, and the `Z` that overwrites it brings the total back. A feed that ends between them evicts the oldest line; the same bytes in one feed keep it. ho-04.2's spec prescribed that feed-end check on the reasoning that the line in progress only grows between commits, which a narrowing overwrite refutes.

The two Notes: **N1**, the resident-size test reads endpoints, not the peak during the feed (Codex's own runs read negative growth), which is the method the spec asked for and is recorded as such; **N2**, foreground codes 30–37 are not each asserted, no CR case combines overwrite with a change of style, and no Panel test selects across an eviction. N2 is taken here as tests; N1 stands.

## The invariant

After every byte the model has read, these hold, and nothing else about what the model reports depends on where a feed ends:

1. The line in progress holds at most 1,048,576 bytes, the newest, whole characters (ho-04.2).
2. The committed lines number at most 10,000, the newest.
3. The bytes retained across the committed lines and the line in progress number at most 10,485,760, the oldest whole lines dropped first.

A ceiling is enforced at the byte that breaks it, and only there. `feed` reads its bytes, then reports; it checks nothing. Compaction of the arrays and release of evicted lines are optimisations that may happen at any moment, feed end included, because they change nothing reported. Every check reads the retained counts, never the physical arrays.

## Solution

**M1 — the aggregate ceiling after every byte.** The check that `feed` makes after its loop moves to the per-byte path: wherever the line in progress gains or loses bytes (`put` and the continuation-byte join), after the origin has moved, evict while the retained bytes exceed the ceiling. `commit()` keeps its eviction. `feed` keeps compaction and the release of evicted lines, and nothing else. The comment that says the line in progress "may have grown since the last commit" goes. How the per-byte check is made cheap is the builder's: a comparison per byte is the floor, and eviction stays amortised O(1) per line as now.

**Tests — equivalence under every partition.** One helper feeds a prefix in 16 KiB chunks and then a tail under every partition the test names, and compares the final `lines`, the final line in progress and `retainedBytes` with the same bytes fed whole. The partitions: 16,384-byte and 1,024-byte chunks of the whole input, and, for the tail, every split point and one byte per feed. The cases, each at a ceiling's edge with a multibyte overwrite:

- the reviewer's probe verbatim: nine 1,048,576-byte lines and one of 1,048,575, then `é\rZ\n`, the tail under every partition. The result under every partition is the invariant's: `é`'s second byte takes the total one byte over the ceiling and the oldest line is evicted at that byte, so ten lines remain, the second first, 9,437,184 bytes retained, whether or not the overwrite shrinks the line again before its LF. (The spec as first committed named the one-feed result of the old code, eleven lines with the first kept; the builder halted on the contradiction and the orchestrator corrected it on 2026-11-05: the invariant rules, the number was the error.)
- the line window: 1,048,575 ASCII bytes, then `é`, CR, `Z`, LF, the tail under every partition;
- the line count: 10,000 lines committed, then `é\rZ\n` twice, the tail under every partition;
- the ceiling-crossing case of ho-04.2 with its overwrites changed to multibyte characters and back, under 16,384 and 1,024.

**N2 — three tests.** Each of SGR 30–37 asserted to its index in one parameterised case; one CR case overwriting a styled span with another style, asserting the spans; one Panel test that fills past the line ceiling, selects a range in the storage, appends enough to evict through it, and asserts the selection and `copy` still describe storage that exists.
